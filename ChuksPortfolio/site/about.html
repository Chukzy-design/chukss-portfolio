About Me

A Little About Me

Frontend Developer & UI/UX Designer — still growing, always building.

I'm Chuks, a Frontend Developer and UI/UX Designer who enjoys turning ideas into digital experiences.

My interest lies where design and technology meet — understanding how something should look, how it should work, and then bringing that experience to life through frontend development.

I'm currently a final-year student, balancing coursework with hands-on project work — including building the frontend and UI/UX for Veda, an AI-powered learning platform, as part of my final year project.

I'm continuously learning, experimenting with new technologies, and building projects that challenge me to become a better developer and designer.

What I Care About

  • Clean interfaces
  • Good user experience
  • Responsive design
  • Maintainable frontend code
  • Continuous learning
  • Solving problems creatively

Alongside design and development, I've also supported remote teams with operations and client communication — experience that shaped how I think about usability from the user's side.
